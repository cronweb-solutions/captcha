# CWCaptcha Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2026**

CWCaptcha is private human-verification and anti-abuse infrastructure operated for websites within the Cronweb network.

This privacy notice explains the limited technical and security information that may be processed when CWCaptcha is used.

---

## Information CWCaptcha May Process

CWCaptcha may process information necessary to provide verification and protect participating websites against automated abuse.

Depending on configuration, this may include:

- IP and network information
- CAPTCHA challenge identifiers
- verification-session identifiers
- request timing
- challenge interaction timing
- verification results
- abuse and rate-limit signals
- browser-security characteristics
- security-event information
- website/domain and requested action

CWCaptcha is designed to avoid collecting information that does not materially contribute to security or verification.

---

## Why This Information Is Processed

Information may be processed for:

- human verification
- automated-abuse detection
- bot mitigation
- fraud prevention
- spam prevention
- replay protection
- rate limiting
- infrastructure protection
- security diagnostics
- investigating suspicious activity

---

## CAPTCHA Challenges

Depending on website configuration and request risk, CWCaptcha may present different verification methods including:

- smart verification
- slider challenges
- image-selection challenges
- procedural visual logic
- ordered-icon verification
- audio verification

Challenge responses are processed to determine whether the requested verification has been completed.

---

## Security and Risk Signals

CWCaptcha may evaluate multiple technical signals rather than relying on one individual characteristic.

Signals may include request frequency, failed verification history, timing behavior, previous security events and local network-abuse history.

No individual signal should necessarily be interpreted as proof that a visitor is malicious.

---

## IP and Network Information

IP addresses and related network information may be processed where necessary for:

- abuse prevention
- rate limiting
- security-event correlation
- network-level attack detection

Retention and anonymization policies may vary according to operational security requirements.

---

## Cookies and Browser State

CWCaptcha may use temporary browser or session state necessary to:

- maintain an active verification
- prevent replay
- associate related challenge requests
- protect the verification process

CWCaptcha does not use this state for behavioral advertising.

---

## Data Retention

Temporary challenges, tokens, sessions and rate-limit information automatically expire.

CWCaptcha includes automated cleanup mechanisms intended to remove expired operational data.

Some security events and audit information may be retained longer where necessary for abuse analysis, incident investigation and infrastructure security.

---

## Third-Party Security Providers

CWCaptcha can support optional external network-reputation or security providers.

Such integrations may be enabled or disabled by the operator.

Where external providers are disabled, CWCaptcha relies on locally operated security and abuse-prevention systems.

---

## Data Sharing

CWCaptcha does not operate as an advertising network and does not sell CAPTCHA interaction data.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or security providers where necessary to operate and protect the service.

---

## Security

CWCaptcha uses technical safeguards intended to protect verification and security information.

These may include:

- encrypted transport
- server-side verification
- short-lived tokens
- replay prevention
- domain binding
- action binding
- request-rate controls
- access controls
- automated expiration and cleanup

No internet-facing system can guarantee absolute security.

---

## Children's Privacy

CWCaptcha is a security and verification service and is not designed to intentionally collect information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

---

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when CWCaptcha's infrastructure, security practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version will remain available in this repository.
